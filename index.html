Our Beginnings

Welcome to Cafe de Melle! We started as a cozy neighborhood space dedicated to serving handcrafted coffee, freshly-baked pastries, and sparkling fruit refreshers.

Baked Fresh Daily

Every morning, our kitchen comes alive with the aroma of buttery European pastries, warm packaged loaf breads, and savory garlic buns.

Comfort in Every Cup

Whether starting your morning in our cafe or grabbing a mid-day iced coffee boost or Harry Chocolate on Grab, we bring care and quality to every order.
